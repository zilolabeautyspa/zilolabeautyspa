Our Story

Born from a love of beauty, culture and community—Zilola Beauty Spa is a sanctuary where every guest is treated like family.

Where Beauty Meets Heart

Zilola Beauty Spa was founded with a simple but powerful belief: beauty is not just about how you look—it is about how you feel. We created a space where every guest, regardless of age or background, can come to be cared for, restored and celebrated.

Our team brings together beauty traditions from across the world—from Japanese head spa rituals to Moroccan oil treatments, Korean glass-hair techniques and Cleopatra-inspired beauty care. We blend time-honored practices with modern expertise to deliver treatments that feel truly transformative.

Located in the heart of Falls Church, Virginia, we proudly serve our diverse and vibrant community. Whether you visit for a quick manicure, a full bridal package or a deeply restorative head spa ritual, we promise to make you feel at home.
